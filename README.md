## 🧪 Selected Research Projects

### 1. KK-LC-1 (CT83) Multi-Epitope Cancer Vaccine Research

Research on the *in silico* design and immunoinformatics characterization of a multi-epitope subunit cancer vaccine targeting KK-LC-1 (CT83).

**Focus areas:** CTL epitope analysis, HLA binding prediction, immunoinformatics evaluation, and computational vaccine design.

*Status: Thesis research; related scientific manuscript in preparation.*

### 2. Structure-Based Drug Discovery Targeting MCR-1 in *Klebsiella pneumoniae*

Research investigating a structure-based computational approach to drug discovery targeting MCR-1.

**Focus areas:** Protein structure analysis, molecular docking, and computational assessment of candidate interactions.

*Status: Manuscript under review.*

### 3. Sugar Transporter Genes in *Plutella xylostella*

Research on genome-wide characterization of sugar transporter genes under chlorantraniliprole stress.

**Focus areas:** Genome-wide identification, sequence analysis, and stress-associated gene characterization.

*Status: Manuscript under review.*

### 4. SnRK2 Genes in *Cajanus cajan*

Research involving the characterization of SnRK2 gene family members in pigeon pea.

**Focus areas:** Gene-family analysis, sequence characterization, and stress-related biological investigation.

*Status: Manuscript under review.*
